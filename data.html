Privacy Policy

BaseComms

Last updated: July 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use BaseComms (the "Application").

BaseComms is a communication and collaboration application based on the open-source Zulip platform. The Application connects users to a dedicated Zulip-based service operated by Polaris Logistic.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name and email address associated with your account.
  • User profile information provided by you.
  • Authentication and account management information.

Communication Data

  • Messages, conversations, files, and content exchanged through the Application.
  • Information related to participation in communication spaces.

Technical Information

  • Device information.
  • Operating system and application version.
  • Application logs related to reliability and security.
  • Information required to prevent unauthorized access.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Application.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Enable communication and collaboration features.
  • Improve reliability and performance.
  • Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Provide technical support.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services required for operation, security, and reliability.

These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples include:

  • Firebase services for application security and device-related functionality.
  • Infrastructure providers used to host and operate the service.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored and processed through the infrastructure used to operate the Application.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Zulip Platform

The Application is based on the open-source Zulip platform.

Some functionality and data processing practices are derived from Zulip technology. Additional information about Zulip privacy practices is available at:

https://zulip.com/policies/privacy

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may request deletion of their information by contacting us.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may request:

  • Access to their personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of personal information.
  • Information about data processing practices.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children without appropriate authorization as required by applicable laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without proper consent.
